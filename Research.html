Overview

I am a researcher specializing in functional nanomaterials, 2D heterostructures, and interfacial kinetics for renewable energy and sensor devices. My work combines synthesis techniques with advanced spectroscopic characterization and high-performance computational simulations.

Currently, my research focuses on scalable synthesis routes, heterojunction band engineering, and charge-transfer mechanisms for catalytic and electronic devices.

Research Focus

Heterojunction Band Engineering

Investigating interfacial band alignments, charge carrier migration kinetics, and surface charge separation to enhance efficiency in catalytic platforms.

Scalable Synthesis & Laser Processing

Developing sustainable, direct-write laser ablation protocols and physical deposition methods on flexible and organic substrate architectures.
